About the Artist

Bee Summers was born and raised in the Philippines but found his way out to finally live his dream life in the US, he was an IT graduate but later in his life he finally pursued modelling and music, which was his ticket to Hollywood, and now he is performing in sold out arenas, like the So-fi stadium and etc. His music is a combination of deep blue melancholia and electronic synth-driven soundscapes, delivering modern moody alternative tracks.
